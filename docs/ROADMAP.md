# FestSphere Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 - Foundation

### Objectives
- Finalize product requirements and design system alignment.
- Set up repository structure and environment standards.
- Define database schema and API contracts.

### Deliverables
- PRD and architecture documentation
- Initial design system handoff
- Git repository structure and coding standards

### Estimated Timeline
- 1 to 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Project scope is approved.
- Engineering team has implementation-ready documentation.

## Phase 1 - Authentication and Core User Management

### Objectives
- Implement secure authentication and user profiles.
- Define role-based access for students, volunteers, faculty, and admins.

### Deliverables
- Signup/login flows
- JWT-based auth
- User profile and role management

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Users can register, log in, and access role-based dashboards.

## Phase 2 - Event Management

### Objectives
- Allow creation, editing, and publishing of festival events.
- Support event categories, schedules, pricing, and visibility.

### Deliverables
- Event CRUD flows
- Event search and filters
- Event detail pages

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Faculty/admins can publish events and students can browse them.

## Phase 3 - Registration and Team Management

### Objectives
- Support individual and team-based registration.
- Manage team creation, invitation, and membership states.

### Deliverables
- Registration flow
- Team creation and join logic
- Validation rules for participant limits

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Users can register individually or as teams successfully.

## Phase 4 - Payments and Tickets

### Objectives
- Integrate online payments and generate QR tickets.
- Ensure secure and idempotent payment handling.

### Deliverables
- Payment gateway integration
- Payment verification flow
- QR ticket generation and download

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Successful registrations trigger payment and ticket generation.

## Phase 5 - Volunteer and Attendance Workflows

### Objectives
- Support volunteer operations and QR verification.
- Provide real-time attendance updates.

### Deliverables
- Volunteer dashboard
- QR scanning flow
- Attendance tracking

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Volunteers can check in participants and update attendance status.

## Phase 6 - Notifications and Certificates

### Objectives
- Deliver announcements, reminders, and certificate generation.
- Improve post-event engagement and completion flow.

### Deliverables
- Announcement engine
- Notification center
- Certificate generation and access

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Users receive notifications and certificates upon completion of relevant flows.

## Phase 7 - Analytics and Administration

### Objectives
- Provide dashboards and reporting for administrators and coordinators.
- Track registrations, revenue, and attendance metrics.

### Deliverables
- Admin dashboard
- Analytics charting and reporting
- Exportable reports

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Admins can monitor platform health and event outcomes.

## Phase 8 - Security Hardening and Performance

### Objectives
- Improve resilience, security posture, and performance under load.
- Perform comprehensive testing and monitoring.

### Deliverables
- Security hardening
- Load and stress testing
- Monitoring and alerting

### Estimated Timeline
- 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Platform passes security review and handles peak traffic expectations.

## Phase 9 - Launch and Scale

### Objectives
- Prepare for public rollout and multi-college expansion.
- Strengthen operational readiness and support.

### Deliverables
- Production deployment
- Documentation handoff
- Support playbooks and launch checklist

### Estimated Timeline
- 1 to 2 weeks

### Acceptance Criteria
- Platform is live, monitored, and support-ready.
